# 🎯 FocusFlow: The Cognitive Architect System

## 🌌 Overview
**FocusFlow** (Onyx Focus) is a professional-grade, local-first productivity engine engineered for elite performance, deep focus, and long-term sustainability. Unlike cloud-dependent tools, FocusFlow treats productivity as a biological and cognitive process, offering an immersive environment that bridges the gap between task management and neurological optimization.

---

## 💎 Design Philosophy
- **Minimalist Tactical Aesthetic**: A high-contrast "Midnight" interface designed to reduce eye strain and cognitive load.
- **Micro-Interaction Excellence**: Smooth, physics-based transitions powered by Framer Motion.
- **Privacy-First Architecture**: 100% offline-capable with SQLite local storage—your data never leaves your machine.
- **Bio-Feedback Integration**: Audio-visual synchronization designed to induce Flow State.

---

## 🚀 Core Features & Mechanics

### 1. The Focus Engine (Neurological Sync)
- **Binaural Beats Synthesizer**: Built-in 40Hz Gamma and 10Hz Alpha oscillators to enhance focus and creativity.
- **Ambient Soundscape Mixer**: 8+ high-fidelity spatial audio tracks (Rain, Forest, Cyberpunk City, Deep Space).
- **Customizable Pomodoro Logic**: Advanced timer presets (Deep Work, Creative Sprint, Rapid Recovery) with native OS notifications.

### 2. Neo AI: Your Cognitive Architect
- **Context-Aware Assistance**: An integrated AI assistant powered by Groq/Llama 3, capable of analyzing your current task list and suggesting optimizations.
- **Dynamic Context Injection**: Neo knows your workload, deadline density, and focus history to provide personalized coaching.
- **Interactive Chat Interface**: Expandable, glassmorphic terminal for real-time strategic planning.

### 3. Professional Analytics (The Productivity Engine)
- **Activity Heatmap**: A high-density visual representation of consistency across a 365-day cycle.
- **Peak Performance Tracking**: Advanced bar charts that identify your "Gold Hours"—the exact time of day when your output is highest.
- **Life Balance Radar**: A specialized RadarChart that visualizes your focus distribution across Health, Career, Finance, Education, and Personal goals.
- **Goal Velocity & Insights**: Dynamic time-filtered (Weekly/Monthly/Yearly) charts that track focused minutes against targets.
- **Weekly Performance Report**: Automated summaries highlighting your "Best Day" and session volume.

### 4. Productivity & Discipline Suite
- **Dynamic Kanban Boards**: High-performance drag-and-drop workflow management.
- **Advanced Habit Tracker**: Discipline-focused tracking with "Streak" mechanics and XP rewards to gamify consistency.
- **Intelligent Recurrence**: Sophisticated task scheduling with custom intervals (Daily, Weekly, Monthly) and precise end-conditions.
- **Daily Focus Goals**: Glowing, real-time progress bars that visualize your journey toward your daily concentration target.
- **Contextual Workspaces**: Zero-latency switching between Personal, Work, and Side Projects to eliminate context switching.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
- **Frontend**: React 19 + Vite + TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 + Framer Motion (Advanced Animations)
- **Intelligence**: Groq SDK / Google Gemini Pro
- **Storage**: SQLite (Better-SQLite3) + Local-First Sync
