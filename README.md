## ✨ Features

### 🚀 Performance & Flow
- **Immersive Deep Work Mode**: Enter a distraction-free fullscreen environment with a built-in **Binaural Beats synthesizer** (40Hz Gamma).
- **Dynamic Timer Presets**: Save and switch between custom `Work / Short Break / Long Break` configurations.
- **Ambient Mixer**: 8+ local high-quality tracks including Rain, Forest, Cafe, and Cyberpunk Space.

### 📊 Professional Analytics
- **GitHub-Style Heatmap**: Visualize your productivity over the last 365 days.
- **Goal Insights**: Track focused minutes and task completion rates across weekly, monthly, and yearly intervals.
- **Peak Performance**: Automatically identifies your most productive hours of the day.

### 🛠️ Productivity Suite
- **Workspaces**: Context isolation for Personal, Work, and Side Projects.
- **Kanban Board**: Drag-and-drop task management for active workflows.
- **Daily Journal**: Markdown-ready scratchpad for thoughts and reflections.
- **Daily Focus Goals**: Glowing progress bars to keep you on track for your daily minutes target.

### 🔒 5-Year Durability & Privacy
- **Local-First**: 100% of your data stays on your machine in a SQLite database.
- **Backup & Restore**: Export your entire history to a portable JSON file.
- **Offline Mode**: Works perfectly without internet; no external APIs or trackers.
- **Task Archiving**: Move completed tasks into a hidden history to keep the UI lightning-fast.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (Recommended 18+)

### 🏁 One-Click Launch (Windows)
1. Double-click `Launch_FocusFlow.bat` in the root folder.
2. The app will open automatically at **http://localhost:3000**.
3. To run invisibly in the background, use `Silent_FocusFlow.vbs`.

### 🛑 Stopping the App
Run `Stop_FocusFlow.bat` to safely shut down the backend and frontend servers.

### ⚙️ Manual Install
1. `npm install`
2. `npm run dev` (Frontend)
3. `node server/index.js` (Backend)
